Your Plex Media YAML

Archipelago joins several players' games into one multiworld. Each player has a slot, and a YAML is one slot's settings file. The owner generates the seed (the shuffled game) from everyone's YAMLs and hosts it as a room.

In Plex Media, your slot is a list of movies, TV episodes, music albums and audiobooks from the owner's Plex server. Your titles start locked, apart from the ones your 3 starting unlocks open (the default). The multiworld sends the other unlocks, and watching or listening sends checks. The bot runs your slot, so playing needs no Archipelago program.

Every title on your list is visible to every player and on the room's public tracker.

The short version

  1. Pick a slot name, 1 to 16 characters.

  2. In the server, send /apmedia yaml name:YourName. Add unwatched:False unless you have accepted a Plex link (below).

  3. Save the file the bot sends you and give it to the owner before they generate.

  4. Once the bot is watching the room, send !ap claim YourName, then /apmedia link slot:YourName.

What you need

  • A slot name: 1 to 16 characters, without {, }, %, a space at either end or a double space, and not Archipelago or another slot's name in the seed.
  • Optional: a Plex link, for watching in Plex to count. Give the owner your Plex user name, and they link it with /apmedia account user:@you plex:<name>. The bot DMs you an Accept button that works for 7 days, so allow DMs from server members (the server's Privacy Settings). Until you accept, only music and audiobooks the bot plays for you count, plus titles you mark with /apmedia check.
  • For the Options Creator only: a PC with Archipelago 0.6.8 or later.

Build it in Discord

  1. In a server channel, type /apmedia, pick yaml, add name with your slot name, and send. The bot picks 15 random movies you have not watched. Add the options below the same way.

  2. Without an accepted Plex link, add unwatched:False. Random movies and shows need the link; albums, audiobooks and titles you name do not.

  3. For another mix, add preset: with tv (3 shows, up to 24 episodes each), music (10 albums), books (3 audiobooks) or mixed (6 movies, 1 show, 4 albums, 1 audiobook).

  4. Or set totals with movies:, shows:, albums: and books:, and name up to 50 titles with titles: (Alien; Halloween (2018); show: Lost S1-2). A count or title given without preset: makes every kind you left out 0: albums:5 gives 5 albums and nothing else.

  5. Add Plex playlists with playlist:, up to 10, separated by ; as in playlist:Road Trip; Lofi: pick one from the suggestions, type ; after it, and pick the next. Once the box fills up, the first ones show as numbers, their Plex rating keys. Each music playlist goes on in its own order, cut into parts of 3 songs, and its unlock opens them one part at a time (Songs per Unlock, below).

  6. The bot replies privately with YourName.yaml and the list's hours, goal and items. A "Not found" line names a title it left out.

  7. Save the file and send it to the owner before they generate. Each build draws new random titles.

!apmedia yaml name=YourName preset=music posts the reply and the file in the channel instead, with options as key=value, quotes around spaces (titles="Alien; Aliens") and more: settings as pairs of their own (strict=true).

Or start in the Options Creator (PC)

  1. Ask the owner to post /apmedia apworld, and save plex_media.apworld from a post that says world 0.4.0. Its lines about "this PC" mean the owner's PC.

  2. With every other Archipelago program closed, choose Install APWorld in the Launcher, pick the file, and reopen the Launcher. A PC with an older copy cannot generate the bot's 0.4.0 YAMLs, and an older form lacks Songs per Unlock, so install 0.4.0 over any older copy.

  3. Open Options Creator, pick Plex Media, and type your slot name under Player Name.

  4. Fill in Your list (the bot picks the titles); pointing at a setting's name shows what it does. A count at -1 follows List: Preset, and List: Preset none keeps only the counts you set and the titles you name. Without an accepted Plex link, turn List: Unwatched Only off. Leave Random? off on List settings.

  5. Change the other Plex Media groups if you like, leave Item & Location Options empty, and choose Export Options.

  6. Attach the file to /apmedia yaml (its file option) or to a !apmedia yaml message. Options you type with it replace the file's.

  7. Send the owner the file the bot returns. Your export can share its name but has no titles, and a seed made with it stops at "this YAML has no titles yet": save the bot's file over it.

After the seed is made

  1. When the owner says the bot is watching the room, send !ap claim YourName, or !ap claim <id> YourName if the bot lists rooms.

  2. Send /apmedia link slot:YourName. The bot answers "Linked YourName on watch #N." and posts a welcome.

  3. /apmedia status shows your progress, goal and items, and /apmedia list your open titles (show:locked or show:done for the others).

Watch open titles in Plex, or play albums, playlists and audiobooks through the bot.

In the bot's voice channel, a song of a playlist part still locked for someone listening is skipped, unless someone there has it open on their list. Plexamp and the other Plex apps play it, and with an accepted Plex link its checks wait for its part to unlock, as a locked title's checks do by default.

Only playback after the seed was generated counts.

Settings you can change

Set them with the /apmedia yaml options shown here, or in the Options Creator. Settings marked more: share one option, split by spaces: more:strict=true trap_percentage=20.

Goal Percent goal-percent:, default 80
The share of the list to finish.
Songs per Unlock more:songs_per_unlock=, default 3, off in the Options Creator
How many songs of each music playlist one unlock opens, 1 to 15: the first copy of the unlock opens part 1, the second part 2. A Wild Ticket opens the part you name. Off puts the playlist in parts of up to 15 songs, each with an unlock of its own. Each part counts as a title toward your goal. To change it, attach your file to /apmedia yaml again with the new number: a lower number typed into the finished file stops the seed from generating.
Timesanity time-movies: 15, time-episodes: 10, time-albums: 0, time-books: 30
Minutes between checkpoints, extra checks at set times inside a title; 0 is off.
Timesanity Strict more:strict=true, default off
On, a checkpoint needs everything before it played. Off, finishing a title earns them all.
Wild Tickets more:wild_tickets=, default 2
Each opens a locked title you choose (/apmedia redeem).
Fast Forwards more:fast_forwards=, default 2
Each earns a title's next checkpoint without playing it (/apmedia ff), or with Timesanity Strict on counts the 10 minutes after where you got to as heard. Never a watch, track or finish.
Remote Controls more:remote_controls=, default 1
Each ends a trap at work on your slot, or blocks the next one.
Snack Bar Trades more:snack_bar_trades=, default 3
Snacks are the filler items that are not traps. Each trade turns 10 of them (more:snack_bar_price=, 5 to 50) into a Fast Forward or a Remote Control (/apmedia snackbar).
Trap Percentage more:trap_percentage=, default 0
The percent of the filler items that come as traps instead of snacks. Five of the seven act only on music or an audiobook the bot plays for you, and everyone in the voice channel hears a music trap. No trap takes away an item or a check.

A short list gets fewer Wild Tickets, Fast Forwards and Remote Controls: 15 movies at the defaults get 2, 1 and none.
